Replace the Contents of Your Model JSON FileOpen the .json file inside your pack at
assets/minecraft/models/block/cyan_glazed_terracotta.json.
Clear out everything inside it and paste this exact text:

json{
  "parent": "minecraft:block/cube",
  "textures": {
    "particle": "minecraft:block/cyan_gt_front",
    "up": "minecraft:block/cyan_gt_top",
    "down": "minecraft:block/cyan_gt_bottom",
    "north": "minecraft:block/cyan_gt_front",
    "south": "minecraft:block/cyan_gt_back",
    "east": "minecraft:block/cyan_gt_left",
    "west": "minecraft:block/cyan_gt_right"
  }
}

assets/minecraft/textures/block/ folder
create and replace the default textures with these 6 custom images:
#TOP_PNG_NAME#.png
#BOTTOM_PNG_NAME#.png
#FRONT_PNG_NAME#.png
#BACK_PNG_NAME#.png
#LEFT_PNG_NAME#.png
#RIGHT_PNG_NAME#.png

Your Texture Files ChecklistCreate your 6 images inside 
assets/minecraft/textures/block/ 
using these exact replacements for 
#COLOR#:#COLOR#_gt_top.png (Top)
#COLOR#_gt_bottom.png (Bottom)
#COLOR#_gt_side_1.png (Faces you)
#COLOR#_gt_side_2.png (Opposite side)
#COLOR#_gt_side_3.png (Your right)
#COLOR#_gt_side_4.png (Your left)

----------

"up" = Top face
"down" = Bottom face
"north" = The side that faces the player (side_1)
"south" = The side opposite the player (side_2)
"east" = The side on your right (side_3)
"west" = The side on your left (side_4)
